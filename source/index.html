The Problem

Some clients arrive every spring with the same messy books.

Missing statements. Uncategorized transactions. Accounts nobody has reconciled since last April. Your team spends its most valuable weeks on cleanup that is hard to bill and harder to schedule.

These clients need monthly bookkeeping, not a once-a-year rescue. Most small firms don't want to staff that work, and the clients won't do it themselves.

Complete Controller fixes it upstream.

Refer those clients to us. We take over the monthly bookkeeping, reconciliations, and reporting. When it's time to file, you log in to books that are already done.

Partner Questions

What CPAs ask before they refer.

Will Complete Controller compete with my firm?

No. We provide bookkeeping and controller services. Tax preparation, planning, and advisory work stays with you, and we send tax questions back to your firm. [CONFIRM: Complete Controller does not offer tax preparation to referred clients]

Can CPAs accept referral fees?

In most cases, yes. The AICPA Code of Professional Conduct requires you to disclose referral fees to your client, and it does not allow commissions from clients you perform attest work for, such as audits and reviews. State board rules vary, so check yours. Our toolkit includes a disclosure letter template. [OPTIONAL: If you would rather not accept a fee, we can apply it as a discount on your client's service.]

How and when are commissions paid?

You earn [RATE]% of each referred client's monthly bookkeeping fees for [TERM] months. Payments go out [PAYOUT SCHEDULE AND METHOD], with a statement showing each client and amount.

Do my clients pay more because I referred them?

No. Referred clients pay the same published rates as every other client. Your commission comes from Complete Controller. [CONFIRM]

Can I see my clients' books?

Yes. With your client's permission, your firm gets access to their hosted books, so you can review the numbers and pull what you need during tax season without waiting on anyone.

What if a client's books are already behind?

That's often why CPAs refer them. We organize and catch up the accounts first, then keep them current every month after that. [CONFIRM: catch-up work pricing]
